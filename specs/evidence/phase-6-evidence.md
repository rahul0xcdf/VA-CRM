### What "blocked" means here

C6.1–C6.3 are sandbox-only by construction. No amount of local testing substitutes for them, and this
document does not claim otherwise. What has been done instead is to put every portal interaction behind
the `IIrpClient` port and drive it with `RecordedIrpServer`, a double that speaks the documented NIC
contract including its error codes and a mid-call timeout. That verifies our side of the conversation.
It does not verify that NIC agrees with our reading of its contract.

**Specifically unverified until sandbox access:** the auth handshake (RSA app key → AES-256 session
key → token refresh), every endpoint path in `NicIrpOptions`, and the exact field names in
`NicInvoicePayload` / `NicEwayBillPayload`. These follow NIC's published schema; the paths and the
certificate are configuration precisely so that correcting them is a settings edit.

## Schema changes

One migration: `20260917050652_AddEinvoicingAndEwayBill`.

Four tables — `EinvoiceRegistrations`, `EinvoiceApiCalls`, `EwayBills`, `EinvoiceSettings` — and six
added columns: `Company.AddressLine1/AddressLine2/City/Pincode`, `Customer.City/Pincode`.

The added columns were **not** in the approved spec and are recorded in §4b with their reason: the IRP
requires a structured address, and Phase 3 met Rule 46 with a free-text print-template header from
which no pincode can be recovered. All six are nullable, so no existing row becomes invalid; the gate
sits at submission, where `EinvoiceService` refuses an incomplete address and names the missing field
(`BuildRequestAsync_WithAnIncompleteCompanyAddress_SaysWhichFieldIsMissing`).

Model guards still hold: no decimal-on-TEXT (money and percentages are INTEGER minor units per
ADR-0003), no cascade deletes, enums persisted by name. `NoBusinessLogicGuardTests` was extended to the
Phase 0–6 approved entity set — it failed on the four new entities until they were declared, which is
what it is for.

## Spec corrections made at approval

1. **IRN concatenation order.** The draft §2 read "Seller GSTIN + Document Number + Document Type +
   Financial Year". The correct order is `SupplierGSTIN + FY + DocType + DocNumber`, no separators, per
   NIC's published worked example. Same four inputs, different order, different hash — and the wrong
   one fails silently, producing a well-formed IRN the portal disagrees with. Corrected in §2 and §4a.
2. **The ten QR fields** were enumerated in §2; the draft only said "its 10 mandatory fields".
3. **§4 open questions** closed into §4a: direct-to-NIC (no GSP), turnover applicability as data,
   the standard NIC Part-B field set, and credentials outstanding.
